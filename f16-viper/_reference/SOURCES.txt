Battery mass: a 4000 mAh 6S LiPo is 630-660 g across common packs
(Turnigy Graphene 658 g, Admiral 45C 630 g); 650 g taken. Flite Test do
not publish a pack mass, so all-up weight is dry 1361 g + 650 g = 2011 g.
