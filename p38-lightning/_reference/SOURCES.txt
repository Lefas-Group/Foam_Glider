Plan sheet: not used. Nothing here is measured off a plan.

Photographs: one only -- the store product image (product 2881, image 9041).
The page carries no second viewpoint, so there is NO cross-view check for
this reconstruction. Mask cut by threshold at <247 on the white background, then closed, and
reduced to the largest component. Holes SMALLER than 400 px are filled;
larger ones are not. A plain binary_fill_holes is wrong for this aircraft:
the rectangle between the two booms is background, and filling it adds
16000 px -- 11.4% of the mask -- that the model would then have to cover.
