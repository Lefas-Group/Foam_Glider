photo  Flite Test store photo, product 3198, undecorated white build.
Direct head-on, camera at about wing height, nose toward the camera. This
is the view that fixes span, dihedral and the spanwise station of each of
the four nacelles; the two inboard nacelles carry the main wheels. The fin,
tailplane and antenna stand above and behind the fuselage. The black nose
cone reads as a broad flat-fronted box, not a smooth ogive. Mask cut from
the image alpha channel.
